AI Sales Agent — Customer Interview Questions

Objective: Understand how coaching and training institutes manage prospective-student enquiries and admissions.

Interview duration: 10–15 minutes
Interview type: Customer discovery
Stage: Before solution validation

Opening

Thank you for your time. I am learning how coaching and training institutes manage enquiries and admissions. I am not selling a product, and I have not assumed that your institute has a problem. I would like to understand your current process and learn from your experience.

May I take notes during our conversation?

Core questions

Could you briefly describe your role and how you are involved in student enquiries or admissions?

Think about the most recent student enquiry you handled. Could you walk me through what happened, from the first contact to the next step?

Where do most enquiries come from — phone calls, WhatsApp, walk-ins, websites, social media or other channels?

What happens when a prospective student asks a question but is not ready to enrol immediately?

How do you keep track of enquiries and remember whom to contact again?

Can you describe a recent occasion when handling an enquiry or following up was difficult? What happened next?

What tools, systems or manual methods do you currently use to manage enquiries and admissions?

If you could change one thing about this process, what would you improve and why?

Useful follow-up prompts

What happened next?

Could you give me a recent example?

How often does this happen?

Who is responsible for that step?

How much time does that typically take?

How do you handle this today?

What happens if nobody follows up?

How do you know whether an enquiry eventually becomes an admission?

Optional quantitative questions

Ask these only if the conversation allows:

Approximately how many new enquiries do you receive in a typical week or month?

Roughly how many enquiries require a follow-up?

How do you track the number of enquiries that become admissions?

Do you have a way to estimate the time spent managing enquiries?

Estimates are acceptable. Do not request confidential student records or personally identifiable student information.

Closing

Thank you for sharing your experience. Is there anything important about your enquiry or admission process that I have not asked about?

Would it be alright if I contacted you again if I need to clarify something?

Interviewer rules

Ask about actual experiences, not hypothetical enthusiasm for a proposed product.

Do not pitch the AI Sales Agent during discovery.

Do not suggest that the institute is losing leads.

Record contradictory evidence as carefully as supporting evidence.

Separate direct observations from assumptions.

Keep interview notes confidential and avoid recording unnecessary personal information.
